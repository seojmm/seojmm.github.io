# Website editing preferences

- Use plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Keep Korean as the default language and the language links in KO / EN order.
- Keep Korean and English content consistent when editing shared information.
- Do not use em dashes (U+2014) or middle dots (U+00B7) in page text, metadata, CSS-generated text, documentation, or responses to the user. Use natural sentences, commas, parentheses, or colons. Use a tilde for Korean date ranges and "to" for English date ranges.
- The awards section is titled "수상 및 장학금" in Korean and "Awards and Scholarships" in English.
