# Website editing preferences

- Use plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Keep English as the default language and the language links in KO / EN order.
- Keep Korean and English content consistent when editing shared information.
- Do not use em dashes (U+2014) or middle dots (U+00B7) in page text, metadata, CSS-generated text, documentation, or responses to the user. Use natural sentences, commas, parentheses, or colons. Use the Korean page as the reference for dates and semester notation in both languages. Use YYYY.MM and a spaced hyphen for month ranges. Translate 현재 as Present in English.
- The awards section is titled "수상 및 장학금" in Korean and "Awards and Scholarships" in English.

- Use the English award and scholarship names and order in the public CV, cv_seojmm.pdf. Use the Korean names, precise dates, and awarding institutions from the supporting resume as recorded in README.md. Use the awarding institutions’ published English names on the English page.
