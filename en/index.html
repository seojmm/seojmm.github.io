I am a master’s student in Computer Science and Engineering at Konkuk University, supervised by Professor Byungkook Oh.

I study how models can better find relevant information by considering relationships among data alongside user context. To this end, I explore graph-based representations of these relationships and knowledge distillation methods that transfer image-derived knowledge to text-based models. I apply these approaches to recommender systems and question answering over knowledge graphs.
