Projects

Development of Multi-Turn Question Answering Technology Based on a Manufacturing-Domain Knowledge Graph

2026.05 - Present

Supported by LG Electronics Production Engineering Research Institute.

Project Member

  • Built a graph retrieval system with Python and Neo4j to find the tables, columns, formulas, and business rules needed to convert manufacturing questions into SQL. Organized the context around tables and improved retrieval to keep required business rules from being left out.
  • Set up model serving with vLLM and built a demo interface in Streamlit. Connected graph retrieval to model inference for interactive question-answering tests and project demonstrations.
  • Knowledge Graph
  • Multi-Turn QA

Recommendation System Model Design Consulting and Case Study

2025.09 - Present

Part of an Agency for Defense Development (ADD) program led by Hanwha Systems.

Project Leader

  • Analyzed entities, relationships, and state changes in command-and-control data. Proposed using user profiles to retrieve candidates within each user’s access permissions, then ranking them by role and interests.
  • Reviewed personalization case studies and designed evaluation criteria for user representation, query analysis, retrieval, and ranking. Proposed evaluating candidate coverage, ranking quality, and performance with limited user histories or incomplete graph information.
  • Recommender Systems

Graph-Structured Interaction for Improving Reliability and Controllability of Multimodal Generative Models

2024.09 - Present

Supported by the National Research Foundation of Korea, funded by the Ministry of Science and ICT.

Project Member

  • Developed a news recommendation method that distills knowledge from images into text-only models.
  • Designed a triple-level supervised fine-tuning method. Used factuality and consistency across news histories for preference alignment.
  • Multimodal Generative Models
  • Graph-Structured Interaction
